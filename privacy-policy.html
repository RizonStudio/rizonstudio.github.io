🔒 Expeniq Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Expeniq is a simple, offline personal money tracker made by Rizon Studio. This page explains in plain words what the app does and what happens to your data.

1. Short summary

  • Expeniq does not collect, upload, sell or share your data.
  • Everything you enter stays only on your own device.
  • No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking. After the first load it works without an internet connection.

2. Your data stays on your device

Everything you enter (transactions, bank and wallet balances, credit card dues, loans, receivables and settings) is stored only on your device, in the app's own browser storage. Rizon Studio has no server and no user accounts. We never receive, see or store your data.

Expeniq is an installable web app. Its files load from rizonstudio.github.io (hosted by GitHub Pages) the first time and are then saved on your device. Like any website, the host may see technical details such as your IP address when the files are downloaded. Rizon Studio does not receive this, and nothing you enter in the app is ever sent to the host.

3. What we do NOT do

  • No email address, phone number or name is asked for.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.
  • No connection to your real bank, UPI or card accounts. You type all amounts yourself.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS or photos.

4. Please do not enter sensitive numbers

Do not type card numbers, CVV, bank passwords, OTPs or UPI PINs anywhere in the app. Expeniq only needs a name (for example "HDFC Credit Card") and an amount. The app blocks entries that look like card numbers.

5. How the PIN works

The 4-digit PIN and security answer only lock the app screen on your device. They are a convenience lock, not strong encryption. The stored data itself is not encrypted, so anyone who can unlock your phone or access the app's storage may be able to read it. Please also use your phone's own screen lock. If you forget the PIN, you can reset it with your security answer. If you also forget the answer, the only way to continue is to clear the app's data, which deletes everything.

6. How the main features work

  • Daily: add income and expenses with a category and payment method (cash, UPI, debit card, credit card). ATM withdrawals and cash deposits only move money between cash and your bank; they are not counted as income or expense.
  • Banks: balances you type for your bank accounts, wallets and other accounts.
  • Loans / Pending: loans, EMIs, credit card dues and money you are to receive. Mark them settled to remove them from your net balance.
  • Report: monthly summary and 6-month chart, calculated on your device.
  • Languages: the app can be shown in several languages. Your language choice is saved only on your device.

7. Backup, Export and Import

Export creates a file (.json) with all your data, and CSV export creates a spreadsheet of your transactions. These files are not encrypted. When you share a file (Drive, WhatsApp, email), it goes only where you choose, so keep it somewhere safe. Import replaces the current data with the backup after you confirm.

8. Reminders and offline use

Due-date reminders are local notifications created on your device when you open the app, and only if you allow notifications. Nothing is sent from a server. After the first load, the app saves its own files on your device so it can work without internet.

9. Losing data

Because data lives only on your device, clearing the app's data or your browser's site data (for example "Clear storage" or "Clear browsing data"), uninstalling the app or changing phone can delete it. Export a backup regularly. We cannot recover your data because we never have a copy.

10. Deleting your data

Delete items inside the app, or clear the app's storage / site data, or uninstall it from your phone settings. This removes everything permanently. Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing to delete on our side.

11. Children

Expeniq is a general-purpose finance tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

12. Changes to this policy

If the app or this policy changes (for example if a new feature handles data differently), the updated version will appear here with a new date.

13. Contact us

Questions about this policy or the app? Email Rizon Studio at rizonstudio.official@gmail.com.

By using Expeniq you agree to this policy.
